About OCR & TTS Engine

Our Mission

Our mission is to make text extraction and speech synthesis accessible to everyone. We combine cutting-edge OCR technology with natural-sounding text-to-speech to create a seamless experience for our users.

Technology

We use a hybrid approach combining Tesseract OCR and EasyOCR for optimal text extraction accuracy. Our text-to-speech engine leverages advanced speech synthesis techniques to deliver natural-sounding audio output.
